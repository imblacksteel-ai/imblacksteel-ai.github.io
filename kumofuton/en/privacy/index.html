Kumofuton Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Summary

Kumofuton does not collect, send, share, or sell your personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your choices on your device only: the last sound you played, the timer length, fade settings, layered sounds and their volumes, and whether to play alongside other apps.

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Internet and external services

The app plays sounds without an internet connection.

When you choose to open a map, a sound's source page on Freesound, the recording request form, or the contact form, it opens in your browser or map app. Those services handle your visit under their own privacy policies.

Recording request and contact forms

These forms are provided with Google Forms. We receive only what you enter. You don't need to enter any personal information. If you include an email address to get a reply, we use it only to reply to you.

Children

Kumofuton does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.

Contact us

Questions, bug reports, and feedback are welcome through our contact form. If you would like a reply, please include your email address.
