Kumofuton Disclaimer

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Not for medical use

Kumofuton plays sounds to help create a restful environment. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, or prevent insomnia or any other condition.

If you keep having trouble sleeping or are worried about your health, please talk to a doctor or another qualified professional.

Volume

Listening at high volume for a long time can damage your hearing. Please keep the volume low, especially with earphones or headphones.

When using the app near babies or young children, keep the volume low and place the device away from their ears.

When playback stops

Playback or the timer may stop because of power-saving features, calls, alarms, other apps, or the operating system.

Kumofuton is not a replacement for an alarm clock. If you need to wake up at a set time, use your device's alarm.

About the recordings

The recordings are processed from sounds that third parties shared on Freesound under CC0 (public domain). Recording places and dates are shown as the recordists described them, and we cannot guarantee that they are accurate.

Some recordings include sounds that suddenly get louder, such as thunder or birds.
